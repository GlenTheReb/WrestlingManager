# Wrestling Manager: product vision

## Player promise

Run a fictional professional wrestling promotion as owner, chief executive, head booker
or a combination. UWF means **Ultimate Wrestling Federation**. Build a company whose
identity, performers and history remain interesting over decades. The creative reference
is the depth and rhythm of a sports-management simulation, with original design, code,
fictional data and visual presentation.

## Product pillars

1. **Creative authority.** The player selects definitive outcomes, finishes, protection,
   competitiveness, interference and aftermath. Results are locked by default. A future
   optional realism setting may permit rare, justified exceptional changes, with evidence.
   Delegating outcomes is voluntary.
2. **Many valid wrestling identities.** Sports, entertainment, hardcore, comedy, lucha,
   technical, strong-style, family, cinematic and character-led promotions serve different
   audiences. Success follows execution and fulfilled expectations, not compulsory card
   quotas or one optimal style. Rebrands change audience composition gradually.
3. **People performing a plan.** Simulate intentions, physical condition, cooperation,
   mistakes, improvisation and crowd responses. Report execution, psychology, engagement,
   storyline advancement, presentation, protection, safety, memorability, broadcast and
   commercial effects separately. Explain causes without displaying hidden equations.
4. **A world with agency and memory.** Rival promotions operate under comparable business
   and talent constraints. People develop, age, form relationships, change employment and
   retire. Record title reigns, contracts, injuries, performances and commercial history.
5. **Depth the player can manage.** Dense readable desktop tables, consistent profile tabs,
   search and comparison, an inbox of meaningful decisions and broad staff delegation.
   Avoid busywork, arbitrary drama and a wall of undifferentiated notifications.
6. **Local ownership and longevity.** Core play works completely offline. Portable saves,
   explicit migrations, reproducible developer seeds and validated fictional content packs
   support long careers. Future optional generated text cannot affect simulation rules or
   make a save depend on a network provider.

## The recurring loop

Read decisions and world news → assess people and programmes → handle creative and
business decisions → build a timed show → watch or simulate performances → understand
consequences → Continue to the next day. Staff can take delegated responsibilities while
the player retains control.

## Long-term systems

Creative work includes modular match finishes and stipulations, reusable angle actions,
character goals, unresolved questions, audience knowledge and earned story beats. Medical
restrictions and live instructions constrain performance without removing creative authority.

People have coherent backgrounds and correlated 1–20 attributes across physical,
in-ring, entertainment and professional groups. Condition is separate from ability.
Scouting reduces uncertainty; it does not grant a universal performance bonus. Prospects
develop through opportunity, coaching, environment and setbacks, not a visible fixed
potential rating. Later portraits must use licensed offline compositing assets.

Business includes contracts and promises, broadcasters, venues, sponsors, ticketing,
merchandise, travel, production, insurance and development. Money problems must follow
legible commitments and cash flow. Locker-room incidents require causes and participants.

Audience cohorts track awareness, affinity, trust, excitement and fatigue by market and
preference. Social reach differs from ticket-selling appeal. Fictional social platforms
and news outlets use deterministic authored templates and aggregate conversations.
External talent includes fictional celebrities and athletes with audience fit, training,
fees, availability and safety constraints.

AI promotions set strategies, plan seasons and programmes, book intermediate events,
react to constraints and review their results. Decisions have logged reasons and cannot
exploit hidden player information. Difficulty comes from information, competition,
people and budgets, not secret rival bonuses.

## Non-goals for the foundation

No full 3D or physics wrestling engine, multiplayer, online backend, cloud account,
licensed real-world roster, final portraits or elaborate animation. No universal match
rating formula, mandatory booking style or runtime language-model dependency. Do not
mistake the first save/overview milestone for the complete initial playable loop.

## Success measures

First prove that a player can book one show, recognise their creative intent in the
performance, understand its consequences and want to continue another week. Then measure
long careers for variety, stability and believable competitor behaviour. Performance
budgets are measurements to collect, not marketing claims.

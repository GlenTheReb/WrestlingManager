# PD-131 — Temporary alumni returns and event rumour rules

Status: Accepted by the owner on 13 September 2026 through PD-134. This records the direction for
temporary legend/alumni appearances and pre-event media speculation. Exact balance, contract variants
and generation rates are execution-ticket calibration rather than open product design.

## Player promise

A major event can feel alive before it happens: credible fictional media may speculate about a return,
and companies can bring back the right alumnus or legend for one event, a short run or a new longer
relationship. A return is an earned company decision, not an automatic nostalgia button.

## People and return agreements

- Alumni, retired workers and legends remain the same persistent Person records with career history;
  `alumnus` means a meaningful past company connection and does not by itself mean retired.
- A temporary return is a date-bound, auditable agreement. It records the company, person, permitted
  role(s), relevant event(s), start/end dates or maximum appearances, public-announcement state and
  the financial/approval record owned by WM-031.
- Permitted roles include guest appearance, promo, manager/authority role, commentary, ceremony,
  trainer/ambassador and a match appearance. Wrestling requires current availability, medical/safety
  clearance and suitable evidenced ability; fame alone never creates a safe active wrestler.
- A one-event appearance can develop into a short run or normal contract through the ordinary
  negotiation workflow. It never silently overwrites retirement, existing obligations or history.
- Custom databases can define alumni, legends, retirement state, prior connections and supplied media
  identities. Deterministic generation only fills missing fictional content.

## Company-dependent eligibility

The player or CPU evaluates a return through company scale and resources, product/brand fit, culture,
event importance, local market, legacy/company connection, current relationship, availability,
audience recognition and risk. A local company can realistically use a regional alumnus; a large
company can pursue broader nostalgia or special-attraction deals. Neither scale nor prestige is a
guarantee, and a poor fit can be costly, rejected or receive a muted reaction.

## Pre-event rumours

- Fictional journalists and outlets can post small pre-event reports about a possible return or other
  public booking development around an upcoming major event.
- The canonical fact is that the outlet made the claim—not that the claim is true. Reports may prove
  accurate, inaccurate or partly accurate when the event resolves.
- A rumour is only generated when the reporter has plausible access, a relevant beat and a reason to
  cover the event. It carries provenance, date, subject/scope, confidence language and later accuracy
  history for that reporter; it must not expose a private plan as confirmed fact.
- A secret can stay secret. The game does not force a rumour for every surprise, and private details
  such as fees or unannounced creative structure remain protected.
- Credible speculation can affect anticipation and media attention; a false report can affect that
  outlet's future trust. It never forces the booking outcome.

## System ownership and boundaries

WM-040 owns alumni/legend/retirement projections and historical identity. WM-031 owns return
agreements, terms and enforceable dates. WM-033 owns the event/calendar commitment; WM-028/029 own
the creative use; WM-008 owns audience/consequence calibration; WM-036 owns rumour, reporting and
social reaction; WM-042 owns the financial impact. Every surface deep-links to the same Person, event
and agreement history.

No real-person allegations, fake real-world reporting, guaranteed pop, forced retirement reversal or
unexplained surprise is permitted. Optional asset packs may add portraits/graphics but cannot change
eligibility, facts or save validity.

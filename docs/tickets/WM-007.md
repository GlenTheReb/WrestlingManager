# WM-007 — Move chains and finish execution

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. Depends on WM-006. Suggested delivery lane: M2.
Reserved branch stem: `feature/wm-007-match-sequences`.

## Player promise

Matches develop through legible sequences—setups, counters, limb work, callbacks and escalation—and
the booked finish is actually attempted with a legal move from the winner's repertoire.

## System contract

- Owns sequence state, transitions/counters, body targets and explicit closing execution.
- Consumes the plan, legal rule context, worker repertoire/proficiency and agent structure.
- Emits attempted/completed/failed/salvaged move facts and finish provenance.
- WM-010 owns catalogue breadth and learning; presentation only renders emitted facts.

## Planned execution slices

1. Sequence and move-attempt facts with deterministic transitions.
2. Setups, counters, reversals, recovery and limb-target state.
3. Signature/finisher selection and locked-result closing execution.
4. Agent/manual beat integration, report explanations and compatibility fingerprints.

## Invariants and exclusions

- The closing move must be legal, known and attributable; no generic finish bypass exists.
- A failed planned move produces explicit recovery, substitution or protected outcome reasoning.
- Body damage, fatigue and proficiency affect execution through documented mappings.
- Do not embed animation, camera or prose instructions in simulation types.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-112 must approve how much sequence detail is player-bookable, finish substitution rules and how protected
failures preserve a result without making every finish guaranteed.

## Capability exit

Representative matches have causal sequence histories and explicit, legal finishes that remain
consistent across full, quick, highlight and resumed simulation.

# WM-036 — Media, press, social and random events

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. Depends on WM-023, WM-028, WM-011 and relevant
WM-030–035 facts. Suggested delivery lane: M4. Reserved branch stem: `feature/wm-036-media`.

## Player promise

Journalists, critics, press conferences and fan communities interpret what actually happened from
different perspectives, while grounded random events create decisions rather than arbitrary punishment.

## System contract

- Owns outlets/critics/fan cohorts, reviews/interviews/scrums, public statements, rumours and event rules.
- Consumes canonical show, relationship, story, business and world facts plus player responses.
- Emits attributed articles/posts, reputation/trust effects and dated decision events.
- It cannot create official match/business facts through prose; celebrity characters have restricted roles.

## Planned execution slices

1. Fact-sourced journalist/critic profiles and show/match reviews.
2. Interviews and press conferences/media scrums with response consequences.
3. Fan/critic social streams, rumours and public statements with knowledge provenance.
4. Preconditions/cooldowns for industry, backstage, travel and business events plus celebrities.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Every factual claim links to stored evidence; opinion/bias is labelled and deterministic.
- Random events have eligibility, seed, cooldown, choice and consequence records.
- Social volume is bounded/deduplicated and cannot become required reading for basic play.
- No live network scraping, real-person defamation or runtime generative model dependency.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-119 must approve outlet/fan-cohort taxonomy, press interaction tone, rumour uncertainty and restricted celebrity
roles/content policy.

## Capability exit

Media and events produce varied but traceable reactions and decisions over representative seasons
without contradicting canonical history or overwhelming the office.

# WM-036 — Media, press, social and random events

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. Depends on WM-023, WM-028, WM-011 and relevant
WM-030–035 facts. Suggested delivery lane: M4. Reserved branch stem: `feature/wm-036-media`.

## Player promise

Journalists, critics, press conferences and fan communities interpret what actually happened from
different perspectives, while grounded random events create decisions rather than arbitrary punishment.

## System contract

- Owns outlets/critics/fan cohorts, reviews/interviews/scrums, public statements, rumours and event rules.
- Consumes canonical show, relationship, story, business and world facts plus player responses.
- Emits attributed articles/posts, reputation/trust effects and dated decision events.
- It cannot create official match/business facts through prose; celebrity characters have restricted roles.
- Custom-database reporters/outlets take priority. Deterministic save generation fills missing media
  roles with persistent fictional people rather than regenerating anonymous reviewers per show.

## Planned execution slices

1. Custom/generated journalist and critic profiles plus fact-sourced reviews of matches, angles,
   promos, storyline developments and whole shows, including outlet-specific star, ten-point,
   letter-grade or prose-only presentation.
2. Interviews and press conferences/media scrums with response consequences.
3. Fan/critic social streams, rumours and public statements with knowledge provenance.
4. Preconditions/cooldowns for industry, backstage, travel and business events plus celebrities.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Every factual claim links to stored evidence; opinion/bias is labelled and deterministic.
- Reporters have persistent region, outlet role, credibility/reach, style/story preferences and a
  small curated set of visible quirks such as harsh grader, story-first, technical purist or local
  advocate. Quirks shape opinion and voice, not canonical outcomes.
- Reviews store one normalized assessment for comparable simulation effects while preserving the
  reporter's public rating scale. WM performance, journalist opinion and fan reception remain
  separate values.
- Every public booking unit can be reviewed with a type-specific rubric. Matches consider execution,
  psychology, engagement, safety and stakes; angles/promos consider performance, story progression,
  clarity/believability and crowd response; show/event reviews consider pacing, card balance, peaks,
  production and value. Booking decisions such as a turn, debut, interference, title change or payoff
  are judged through logic, protection, momentum and story impact.
- Reporters can publish positive, mixed, sarcastic, blunt or strongly negative posts and can reply to
  other public accounts when their voice and interest justify it. Authored language remains varied
  but does not use repetitive abuse, slurs or real-person allegations.
- A reporter only reacts to public material they could observe through attendance, coverage or an
  available broadcast. Private booking plans never become fact through a post; uncertain claims use
  explicit rumour provenance.
- Pre-event event rumours can cover a possible temporary alumni/legend return. The post records its
  outlet, access, scope and confidence language; its later accuracy affects reporter trust but never
  forces the booking or exposes a private agreement as confirmed fact.
- Random events have eligibility, seed, cooldown, choice and consequence records.
- Social volume is bounded/deduplicated and cannot become required reading for basic play.
- No live network scraping, real-person defamation or runtime generative model dependency.
- Procedural posts combine authored structures with persistent account voice, knowledge, bias,
  relationships, region and canonical event facts. Player-directed and independent worker/company
  posts can have management, story, sponsor, contract or kayfabe consequences through their owners.
- News cards and articles use relevant available imagery: worker portrait first for a worker story,
  then company mark, show/venue art or category artwork. A consistent silhouette/icon is the fallback;
  irrelevant decorative pictures are omitted. Optional assets never affect facts or save validity.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-119 has approved the lightweight social-feed direction. It must still approve the complete outlet/
fan-cohort taxonomy, press interaction tone, rumour uncertainty, random events and restricted celebrity
roles/content policy. PD-131 supplies the accepted boundary for temporary-return speculation.

## Capability exit

Media and events produce varied but traceable reactions and decisions over representative seasons
without contradicting canonical history or overwhelming the office.

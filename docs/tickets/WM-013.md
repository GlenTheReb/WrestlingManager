# WM-013 — Presentation event contract

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. A match-view slice may follow WM-007; full
completion integrates WM-029, WM-034, WM-036 and WM-038. Reserved branch stem:
`feature/wm-013-presentation-contract`.

## Player promise

Every way of watching or recapping a show tells the same official story because all presentation is
derived from one versioned stream of canonical facts and non-authoritative cues.

## System contract

- Owns renderer-neutral presentation event envelopes, ordering, references and version policy.
- Consumes simulation, angle, broadcast, media and CPU-show facts without mutating them.
- Emits data for full, quick, highlight, instant, recap and future accessible renderers.
- Carries instruction queued/sent/delivered/responded/applied/failed facts, the semantic referee
  opportunity used and the resulting canonical plan amendment without making presentation authoritative.
- WM-014 owns guaranteed text/2.5D rendering; conditional WM-043 may consume the same stream for 3D.
  Neither renderer nor text/cue templates own canonical outcomes.

## Planned execution slices

1. Match event envelope mapped from existing `SimEvent` facts with golden compatibility fixtures.
2. Sequence/finish, tag/multi-person and stipulation event extensions.
3. Structured angle/dialogue/entrance/interference events.
4. Broadcast/media/CPU-show metadata, compaction and version compatibility.
5. Live-direction lifecycle, immutable executed-prefix and amended-future compatibility fixtures.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Official participants, result, incident and consequence facts are identical in every view mode.
- A player-authorised finish or winner change becomes the new canonical plan only after authoritative
  simulation accepts it; reports preserve both the original brief and dated amendment.
- Cues may suggest lighting, emphasis or pace but cannot decide gameplay.
- Missing optional fields/assets degrade gracefully and never invalidate a save.
- Do not add renderer libraries, theme audio, titantrons or character animation here; WM-043 remains
  optional downstream work behind its own feasibility gate.
- The presentation contract supports a concise result summary and a detailed chronological report.
  Both can reference show date/type/brand, city, venue, attendance/capacity, distribution, total
  duration, match/segment order, participants, stipulation/title context, result/finish, elapsed time,
  show-context rating dimensions, crowd progression, incidents and consequences when their owning
  systems provide those facts.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-122 must approve event-retention detail, compatibility promises and which cues are stored versus derived.

## Interface research starting points

At execution, recheck current sources rather than copying them. Useful patterns include
[AEW's chronological results format](https://www.allelitewrestling.com/post/aew-dynamite-blood-guts-results)
for date, arena/city, broadcast context and narrative;
[WWE's quick hits plus full details](https://www.wwe.com/shows/raw/2026-07-27); and
[Cagematch's structured event record](https://www.cagematch.net/?id=1&nr=443495) for event type,
location, arena, attendance, match time and explicitly external ratings. WM must improve navigation
and explanation and must keep its simulation rating separate from journalist or fan opinion.

## Capability exit

Player and CPU shows can produce versioned, bounded streams whose detailed and condensed consumers
cannot contradict official history.

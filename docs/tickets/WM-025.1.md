## Outcome and player value

Replace the legacy single worker name with canonical Person, Character and dated Character Tenure
identity; make ring names, aliases, masks, gimmicks, alignment intent, public knowledge and character
changes persistent and coherent across profiles, Talent Search, booking/history references and old
saves.

## Intentionality answers

- **Player decision:** propose and prepare a rename, turn, mask/presentation adjustment, retirement,
  revival or complete character reboot with visible acceptance/readiness/risk before public debut.
- **Canonical cause:** Person/Character/Tenure facts plus personality, trust, adaptability, company
  context, exposure, preparation and performed public events.
- **Consequence and explanation:** character state, comfort, acceptance, perception, identity
  knowledge and dated history change; advice/history retain the inputs and outcome.
- **State layers:** Person and employment facts remain real-life; Character/Tenure/change plans are
  kayfabe; UI copy and later visuals are presentation.
- **Reused contract:** extend current Worker/Profile and WM-021 discovery projections; do not create a
  parallel roster, search engine or identity-only worker record.
- **Missing information:** imported genuinely unknown legal names remain unknown; private
  plans/identity links stay hidden. Current WM-generated schema-7 workers migrate their existing full
  name to both legal name and initial ring name, matching the owner's explicit migration decision.
- **Main abuse/failure case:** overlapping active identities, stale/repeated change commands, history
  rewriting, hidden-name leakage or a presentation event mutating real health/contracts.
- **Workflow improvement:** wrestling surfaces lead with the active ring name; the detailed profile
  separates Character/Presentation from Personal facts; change review shows consequences and links
  directly back without losing Finder state.
- **Integration:** active identity flows into Finder, profile, booking labels, news/history snapshots
  and generated contracts while later teams/titles/stories remain outside this ticket.

## Acceptance criteria

- A Person may have dated open identities in different companies and a validated concealed alternate;
  lists/bookings use the correct ring name for company/date while the authorised profile links history.
- Active and former permitted aliases are SQL-searchable with an explanation, bounded pagination and no
  full-world scan; private/unknown legal names never appear in results.
- A before-debut rename is low-risk; an exposed abrupt rename and a prepared post-break rename produce
  distinguishable explained forecasts without changing wrestling stats.
- Gimmick advice derives from the creative brief and available worker/context evidence; unavailable
  future product/audience inputs are identified rather than fabricated. No direct stat mutation or
  editable numeric bonus exists.
- Intent and dated response evidence can represent successful intended villain boos, rejected hero
  boos, mixed and indifferent reactions without creating a universal perception score.
- Accepted changes do not become public until the selected public event completes; retry/resume cannot
  duplicate the change or its history.
- Character retirement/revival does not retire the Person or rewrite earlier matches.
- Schema-7 saves migrate once, preserve existing identities/history and resume deterministically;
  invalid/modded records fail with precise diagnostics and no partial mutation.
- Existing booking, simulation, relationships, inbox/news and WM-021 Finder behavior remain functional.

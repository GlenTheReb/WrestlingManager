# WM-030 — Player career, ownership, goals and company hierarchy

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. Depends on WM-011; a thin owner/role slice serves
M1. Reserved branch stem: `feature/wm-030-company-governance`.

## Player promise

The player creates a believable management persona whose background affects initial reputation,
knowledge, relationships and perception, then works within explicit company authority and goals.

## System contract

- Owns the player Person/career profile, management background/qualifications, starting reputation,
  role tenure, owners/boards/executives/bookers, reporting lines, permissions, goals and trust.
- Reuses WM-022 personality/traits, WM-023 relationships and WM-025 identity rather than creating a
  player-only personality model; consumes company identity/culture and later finance/performance facts.
- Emits authorization decisions, evaluated goals and dated governance changes.
- Kayfabe authority remains WM-026; corporate ownership edges also feed WM-012/037.

## Planned execution slices

1. Player identity, portrait/biography, personality, professional background, qualifications,
   experience, rare family/industry connections and contextual starting perceptions.
2. Starting role, owner and permission model with current-career migration.
3. Measurable owner/company goals and explainable evaluation.
4. Boards, executives, booking teams, inner circles and figureheads.
5. Conflict, succession, dismissal/resignation and career/governance history.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Permissions derive from dated roles; hierarchy changes never silently seize control.
- Goals cite measurable facts, time windows and evaluation rules.
- Real reporting authority cannot be inferred from an on-screen character.
- Background choices set evidence-based starting conditions, not permanent magical bonuses. Positive,
  neutral and negative personality choices require balanced creation rules and remain part of the
  same event-driven trait system used by every Person.
- Perception is contextual by observer/market (for example credibility, respect or notoriety), never
  one universal score and never a substitute for stored relationships or reputation.
- Do not add finance rules or CPU strategy directly.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-103/104 and PD-134 rules B5–B9 and T1 lock player creation, perception, playable-role and governance
direction. Each execution ticket must define its bounded background/qualification catalogue,
permission matrix, migration and explanation fixtures before coding.

## Capability exit

Different company structures create legible authority and goal tradeoffs, and every control decision
or role change is explainable from governance history.

# WM-033 — Calendar, events and live operations

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. Depends on WM-011, WM-031–032 and WM-042.
Suggested delivery lane: M4. Reserved branch stem: `feature/wm-033-events-touring`.

## Player promise

The company operates on a real calendar where venues, travel, local demand, ticket prices, event
types and worker availability make scheduling a strategic decision.

## System contract

- Owns calendar/event series, venue bookings, travel/tours, ticketing plan and live-operation costs.
- Consumes company/brand/geo, contracts/availability, finance, production and audience demand.
- Emits dated show commitments, conflicts, attendance/gate facts and operational history.
- Show-card creative content remains booking; broadcast rights are WM-034.

## Planned execution slices

1. Calendar, recurring event-series and availability/conflict validation.
2. Venue/capacity/local demand/ticket price and reconciled gate/costs.
3. Tours, travel/weather and regional progression.
4. House/developmental events with auto, agent-directed and light-play modes.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Attendance and gate cite capacity, demand, price, region, card/product and distribution factors.
- Scheduling cannot double-book a worker/venue without an explicit supported resolution.
- House shows affect appropriate reps, chemistry, condition and local popularity without TV weighting.
- Do not fabricate real venues, legal regulations or weather effects without approved data/rules.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-116 must approve calendar granularity, event families, travel/weather depth, ticket-price controls and house-show
automation consequences.

## Capability exit

Representative TV, special, premium, developmental and house schedules validate, operate and
reconcile with worker availability, history and company finance.

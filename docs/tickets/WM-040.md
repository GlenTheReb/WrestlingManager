# WM-040 — Legacy, records and honours

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. Depends on WM-027 and WM-036–039. Suggested
delivery lane: M5. Reserved branch stem: `feature/wm-040-history-honours`.

## Player promise

Long careers produce browsable show/company/career history, alumni, legends, awards, rankings,
records and Hall of Fame recognition derived from what actually happened.

## System contract

- Owns derived records, award/honour rules, historical indexes and correction/rebuild policy.
- Consumes canonical dated match/show/title/team/company/person/media events.
- Emits explainable rankings, nominations/selections and navigable historical projections.
- Source events remain authoritative; honours cannot rewrite them.

## Planned execution slices

1. Show/company/career timelines and historical navigation.
2. Alumni/legend/retirement and title/team record projections.
3. End-of-year awards, Top 100s and transparent ranking criteria.
4. Hall of Fame, record correction/rebuild and long-save query/performance evidence.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Derived records rebuild from canonical events and identify corrections/supersessions.
- Names, characters, teams and ownership changes preserve identity-at-time and current deep links.
- Ranking and award criteria are visible; no secret prestige total decides everything.
- Do not manufacture missing historical detail or use presentation prose as evidence.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-123 must approve award/ranking/Hall of Fame criteria, nomination cadence and historical retention/detail targets.

## Capability exit

Representative long careers retain accurate, performant and explainable histories/honours through
identity changes, corrections and database/save upgrades.

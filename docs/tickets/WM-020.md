# WM-020 — Wrestler stat hierarchy

Status: Implemented and verified on 10 September 2026 on
`feature/wm-020-wrestler-rating-system`; uncommitted pending owner review. WM-008 later consumes
and calibrates the accepted simulation mapping. Execution record: `docs/tickets/WM-020.1.md`.

## Outcome and scope

Replace the shallow wrestler-rating presentation with a FIFA-like hierarchy: exactly six visible
base stat groups, each scored as an integer from 0 to 100 and explained by its own sub-stats.
The base ratings must be meaningful summaries of the sub-stats, not six unrelated decorative
numbers. The same canonical data must be used by content generation, save data, simulation and
the roster/profile UI.

The owner has selected the six group names, canonical sub-stat catalogue, deterministic derivation,
style taxonomy, Archetype rules and current-Archetype Overall. This is not a context-free universal
rating: it measures effectiveness in the worker's current trained style and can change as attributes,
moveset readiness, mastery and evidenced performance change.

## Canonical catalogue — accepted as PD-008

All base groups and sub-stats are integer values from 0 to 100.

- **Movement:** Acceleration, Ring Speed, Agility, Acrobatics, Jumping, Flexibility, Balance.
- **Physicality:** Strength, Stamina, Toughness, Recovery, Injury Resistance.
- **Ringcraft:** Technical Grappling, Chain Wrestling, Striking, Brawling, Submissions, Aerial
  Wrestling, Power Offense, Hardcore/Weapons, Countering.
- **Psychology:** Match Storytelling, Pacing, Selling, Crowd Reading, Match Calling, Adaptability.
- **Fundamentals:** Timing, Consistency, Safety, Bumping, Positioning, Cooperation.
- **Entertainment:** Presence, Microphone, Character Performance, Crowd Connection.

Explosiveness, schedule resilience, fatigue, injury risk and style-specific overall ratings are
derived outputs, not editable sub-stats. Look, popularity, menace, marketability and reputation are
separate non-ability concepts. Similar fields must preserve clear boundaries: raw Acrobatics versus
applied Aerial Wrestling, raw Strength versus applied Power Offense, and individual Technical
Grappling versus fluid, opponent-linked Chain Wrestling.

## Accepted derivation and style model — PD-101

A visible base-group score is the arithmetic mean of that group's canonical sub-stats, rounded to
the nearest integer. Sub-stats remain canonical; group scores are derived projections.

`Discipline Fit = 55% Attribute Fit + 25% Moveset Readiness + 15% Discipline Mastery + 5% Proven
Performance`.

With one Secondary, `Discipline Blend = 75% Primary + 25% Secondary`. With two, it is
`70% Primary + 20% Secondary 1 + 10% Secondary 2`; without one, it is 100% Primary.

`Current-Archetype Overall = 50% Discipline Blend + 20% Psychology base + 20% Fundamentals base

- 10% Entertainment base + Proven Performance Modifier`. The modifier is an evidence-backed integer
  from -3 to +3 and cannot come from popularity, Look, booking position or company size.

### Assignment and stability rules

- Highest credible Discipline Fit is Primary.
- A Secondary needs Fit 65 or higher, must be within 12 points of Primary, and needs Moveset
  Readiness and Discipline Mastery of at least 60. Retain at most two.
- Best Fit below 55 produces Developing rather than a confident Archetype.
- A new Primary must lead the current Primary by four points for 90 in-game days or eight matches;
  an eight-point lead may change it immediately.
- All-Rounder requires five Fits of at least 85, Readiness and Mastery of at least 80 in all five,
  and no more than five points between the first and fifth Fits.
- Age, fatigue and injury affect current performance and availability, not historical style identity.

### Approach, Specialisations and naming

Approach stores five independent dimensions: Tempo (Methodical, Balanced, Fast-Paced), Structure
(Traditional, Escalating, Spot-Driven), Presentation (Sport-Realistic, Dramatic, Spectacle, Comedy),
Contact (Light, Standard, Stiff) and Risk (Conservative, Balanced, Daredevil).

Specialisations are displayed as badges:

- Technical Grappling: Chain Wrestling, Catch Wrestling, Mat Control, Counter Wrestling.
- Submission Wrestling: Joint Locks, Chokes, Stretch Holds, Limb Targeting.
- Lucha Libre: Ropework, Lucha Grappling, Lucha Sequences, Multi-Person Flow.
- Aerial Wrestling: Diving, Springboards, Slingshots, Aerial Counters.
- Striking: Punches, Kicks, Knees/Elbows, Chops/Forearms.
- Power Wrestling: Lifts, Presses, Impact Slams, Throws.
- Brawling: Clinch Fighting, Dirty Fighting, Arena Brawling.
- Hardcore Wrestling: Weapons, Tables/Ladders, Environmental Offense, Deathmatch.
- Shoot Fighting: Takedowns, Ground Control, Ground-and-Pound, Martial-Arts Integration.

Archetype naming uses a curated rule table rather than generated prose. Primary supplies the noun;
the strongest Secondary normally supplies the modifier; a distinctive Approach may replace it.
Specialisations remain badges. Valid examples include Lucha Aerialist, Submission Technician, Power
Brawler, Shoot Striker and Technical Striker. Custom databases may supply validated name overrides.

### Training and role boundary

Style training changes relevant sub-stats, Moveset Readiness, Discipline Mastery and moveset content
over time. Forecasts show projected gain/loss and confidence; trainer quality improves development and
forecast confidence but never guarantees a result. Height, weight, reach and body type constrain move
feasibility and safety rather than adding free points.

PD-009 uses one Person record for wrestlers and non-wrestling professions. WM-032 owns Shared Person
attributes, Profession Skills and `Role Rating = 65% Profession Skills + 20% Shared Person attributes

- 15% Role Experience`. WM-020 must preserve historical wrestler ability for later staff careers and
  must not create fake wrestling stats or Overall for people without evidenced wrestling ability.

## Acceptance criteria

- A documented canonical catalogue contains exactly six base groups, their named sub-stats,
  integer 0–100 bounds and derivation/display rules approved by the owner.
- Generated wrestlers, DTOs, profile UI and roster data use the same catalogue. Values outside the
  allowed range are rejected at every persistence boundary.
- Simulation consumes only the explicitly mapped sub-stats, preserving determinism and recording
  any intended behavioural changes in focused tests.
- Existing careers and paused shows are migrated through a versioned, backup-safe save migration;
  no old field is silently reinterpreted as a new editable source of truth.
- Profile UI makes base ratings, sub-stats, Discipline Fits, Archetype, Approach and the explicitly
  labelled current-Archetype Overall inspectable without presenting it as universal truth.
- Tests cover bounds, derivation, generated content, save migration/resume and representative
  simulation effects.

## Capability exit

All workers, saves, simulation mappings and management projections use one approved six-group model;
ratings are bounded and explainable, and current Overall remains style-specific and causally separate
from popularity, business value, potential and Profession Role ratings.

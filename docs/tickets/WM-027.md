# WM-027 — Championships, divisions, rankings and tournaments

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. Depends on WM-026. Suggested delivery lane: M3.
Reserved branch stem: `feature/wm-027-titles-rankings`.

## Player promise

Championships and competitive structures have eligibility, prestige, contender logic and complete
lineage, making wins and booking decisions meaningful over time.

## System contract

- Owns titles, reigns/defences/vacancies, divisions, ranking snapshots and tournament brackets.
- Consumes official results, characters/teams, company/brand eligibility and dated events.
- Emits contender/title context for booking, storylines, profiles, media, awards and history.
- Match outcomes remain owned by simulation; a title system applies each official result once.

## Planned execution slices

1. Title/reign/defence lineage with result-driven transitions and vacancy handling.
2. Division/brand eligibility and contender picture.
3. Explainable company/world rankings with dated snapshots.
4. Tournament brackets/scheduling integration and complete historic navigation.

## Invariants and exclusions

- A result can change a title once; reload or duplicate news cannot reapply it.
- Eligibility and ranking movement expose the rules/facts responsible.
- Character/team/company changes preserve the historical holder identity at the time.
- Do not book matches automatically or invent ceremonial presentation facts.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-134 rules M1–M6 lock titles, prestige, rankings and tournament direction. The execution ticket must
define versioned formulas and representative bracket/division fixtures.

## Capability exit

Titles, rankings and brackets remain auditable and consistent across results, vacancies, company/
character changes, saves and database upgrades.

# WM-023 — Wrestler relationships and interactions

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. Depends on WM-022 and PD-104. Reserved branch
stem: `feature/wm-023-relationships`.

## Outcome and scope

Add persistent wrestler-to-wrestler relationships and player interaction options that make those
relationships visible and consequential. Relationship state must model each wrestler's point of
view explicitly when the design calls for it; it cannot assume that every feeling is symmetric.
The player needs to see meaningful history and choose context-appropriate interactions from
wrestler profiles or relevant management screens.

The owner has not selected relationship categories, scales, interaction catalogue, costs,
cooldowns, success rules or consequences. This ticket must capture those product rules before
building mechanics.

## System contract

- Owns directional personal relationship state, dated memories and player-interaction resolution.
- Consumes identity/personality/language plus canonical shared events through explicit mappings.
- Emits relationship changes, interaction history and office/news facts; kayfabe/company sentiment
  remain separate records owned by WM-025/012.

## Planned execution slices

1. Resolve PD-104 and define directional relationship/memory contracts.
2. Deterministic relationship generation, persistence and history projection.
3. Contextual interaction eligibility, cooldown/cost and outcome resolution.
4. Profile/office/news integration plus asymmetric and repeat-protection tests.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-104 must define relationship categories, bounds, memory/decay and interaction outcomes.

## Acceptance criteria

- An owner-approved relationship model defines categories, directionality, value bounds, history
  and the events that can create or change a relationship.
- Personal relationships, kayfabe relationships and worker-to-company sentiment are distinct typed
  records; an angle, team or storyline never silently rewrites a real personal relationship.
- Relationships and interaction history are persisted, validated and migrated without damaging
  existing careers or paused shows.
- The player can inspect the relevant relationship context and select only interactions valid for
  the current situation; outcomes are recorded in the appropriate history/news surfaces.
- Interaction resolution has explicit, testable inputs and outcomes. Trait or language effects are
  used only when their rules are documented; there are no hidden random modifiers.
- Tests cover asymmetric relationships where supported, invalid actions, persistence/reload,
  repeat/once-only protection and representative consequences.

## Capability exit

Directional relationships and player interactions remain causal, auditable and distinct from
kayfabe/company sentiment across saves, profiles, news and repeated actions.

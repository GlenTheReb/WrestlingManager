# WM-025 — Real-life and kayfabe separation

Status: Implemented, review-fixed and merged through PR #6 at `61d2ec1`. Depends on WM-022–023 and
accepted PD-106; its core identity model precedes full WM-024 profile integration.
Suggested delivery lane: M1–M3. See
[WM-025.1](WM-025.1.md) for the implementation contract and local verification record.

## Player promise

A wrestler can have private personal reality and one or more public wrestling identities without a
heel turn, mask, storyline injury or promo claim corrupting contracts, health or relationships.

## System contract

- Owns character identity, ring names, gimmicks, alignment, masks, public claims and dated tenures.
- Consumes person identity and company/brand context.
- Emits explicit kayfabe references for booking, news, profiles and history.
- Real health/contracts/relationships remain owned elsewhere; presentation only reports a chosen plane.

## Combined execution scope

WM-025.1 delivers Person-versus-character identity/migration; gimmick, alignment, mask and tenure
state; turns, debuts, retirement/revival and public knowledge; and profile/search/booking/news/history
integration in one coherent branch with internal phases and one final review.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Every event declares whether it describes real-life, kayfabe or presentation state.
- One person may hold historical/multiple context-bound characters, but active-tenure rules validate.
- Character changes never rewrite personal relationships or official match identity history.
- Do not implement storylines, teams, titles or angle scripting here.

## Accepted decisions

PD-106 is decision-complete in
[character-and-presentation-rules.md](../character-and-presentation-rules.md). It includes rename
timing risk, contextual gimmick effects without stat buffs, explicit launch/readiness, open and secret
multiple identities, character retirement/revival, alignment intent and separate audience perception.
Legacy `Worker.name` becomes the first active ring identity. For current WM-generated saves it also
becomes the initial legal name; genuinely unknown imported legal names remain unknown.

## Capability exit

People, characters and their histories remain separately identifiable and consistent across saves,
companies, profiles, booking, news and future custom databases.

# WM-022 — Wrestler identity and personality

Status: All four WM-022 slices implemented together; final verification is recorded in
[WM-022.1 execution record](WM-022.1.md). Committed and pushed; PR pending. Branch:
`feature/wm-022-wrestler-identity`.

## Outcome and scope

Give every wrestler a richer persistent identity: personality traits, spoken languages, hobbies
and a readable biography. The information must be generated consistently for fictional workers,
survive saves and be visible where the player makes staffing and booking decisions.

The initial data model must distinguish factual profile information from systems that change game
outcomes. Personality or language should not silently alter match simulation, contract outcomes
or interactions until their causal rules are designed and tested.

## System contract

- Owns factual person identity, deterministic biography inputs, languages, hobbies and personality data.
- Emits validated profile facts and explicitly approved trait/language factors for downstream systems.
- Characters/gimmicks are WM-025; relationships are WM-023; prose cannot invent canonical identity.

## Delivered execution slices

1. Validated identity catalogues and deterministic, explained personality descriptions.
2. Independently seeded generation and backup-safe schema-5 persistence, including paused shows.
3. Person & traits profile tab, factual biographies and basic language-aware roster text search.
4. Reusable event-evidence evaluator, corrections, lifecycle history and daily expiry. Producers and effects remain
   with relationships, contracts/wellness, media, broadcasting and history capabilities.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-103 documents vocabulary, representation, naming, evidence and causal boundaries. The owner's
combined implementation instruction adopts its thresholds as rule-version-1 defaults, not proven balance.
PD-102 and PD-134 now lock scouting and sensitive-system direction. Downstream execution tickets still

## Acceptance criteria

- A documented, owner-approved catalogue defines trait vocabulary, language representation, hobby
  data and biography inputs, including which elements are generated versus stored.
- Newly generated workers receive internally consistent identity data from deterministic content
  generation; existing saves upgrade through a versioned, backup-safe migration.
- Worker profiles present the information clearly without pretending it has gameplay effects that
  have not been implemented.
- Typed DTOs and validation prevent malformed or contradictory identity data from entering saves.
- Tests cover deterministic generation, persistence/reload, migration, profile presentation and
  validation failures.

## Capability exit

Every wrestler has coherent persisted identity data, and each downstream gameplay effect is either
explicitly approved/tested or honestly absent from the interface.

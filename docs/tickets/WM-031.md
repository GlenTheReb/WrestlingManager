# WM-031 — Contracts, promises and talent operations

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. A basic M1 negotiation slice depends on WM-022,
WM-030 and WM-042; relationship effects later consume WM-023. Reserved branch stem:
`feature/wm-031-talent-operations`.

## Player promise

Hiring, retaining and releasing talent is a negotiation between parties with goals, leverage,
obligations and memory—not a one-click price check.

## System contract

- Owns offers/contracts/clauses, negotiation state, promises, absences/suspensions and wellness cases.
- Consumes people/personality/relationships, governance permissions, finance budgets and company fit.
- Emits enforceable obligations, dated negotiation/promise history and office/news events.
- Employment does not own personal relationships, medical simulation or company accounting entries.

## Planned execution slices

1. Basic offer/renewal/release with wages, dates, exclusivity and budget commitment.
2. Negotiation rounds, goals/leverage, refusal/counter and expiry.
3. Options, bonuses, appearance/merchandise/creative-control/non-compete clauses and temporary
   alumni/legend return agreements with date, event/appearance cap, role, approval and expiry.
4. Promises, persistent morale and live-booking memories, time off, loans, absences, suspensions and
   configurable wellness/drug-testing cases.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Terms become binding only through an accepted, valid contract transition.
- Every promise has scope, due date, status and outcome; reload cannot resolve it twice.
- Pre-match disputes, forced decisions, unsafe requests, humiliating changes and protected emergency
  choices consume factual PD-112 evidence. They may affect morale and promises contextually but cannot
  create generic penalties merely because a live instruction occurred.
- Contract overview reconciles with WM-042 committed cash and actual ledger payments.
- A temporary return cannot overwrite retirement, another contract, availability, medical clearance or
  historic identity. It remains a distinct auditable agreement and may later convert only through a
  normal negotiated contract transition.
- Sensitive systems are fictional, configurable and non-exploitative; do not simulate real diagnoses.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-103–104 and PD-134 rules N1–N7, Q6–Q9 and U4 govern negotiation, personality, promises, money,
wellness and temporary returns. Execution tickets own bounded clause catalogues and balance fixtures.

## Capability exit

Offers, contracts and promises are enforceable and auditable across expiry, absence, release and
company changes, with clear leverage, risk and financial consequences.

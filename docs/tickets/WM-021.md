# WM-021 — Talent Search and company Roster discovery

Status: Incremental capability. Current-data Talent Search is implemented in
[WM-021.1](WM-021.1.md) and merged through PR #5 at `28f6a26`; WM-025 subsequently supplied active
ring identities and aliases. The distinct player-company Roster workspace remains as
[WM-021.2](WM-021.2.md). Multi-entity Global Search remains WM-039.

## Outcome and scope

Make the world practical to explore at game scale through two clear products that reuse one bounded
discovery foundation: Talent Search for finding/recruiting people across the known world, and Roster
for operating the player's current company. Talent Search offers live text search, composable
filters, stable sorting, comparison, saved views, shortlists and blacklist without loading the
entire world into the browser or Rust. Roster favours fast company-scoped status, sorting and actions
instead of exposing recruitment filters by default.

TEW IX is a research reference, not a screen specification. The accepted
[PD-102 rules](../search-and-discovery-rules.md) retain detailed worker filtering and improve it with
a persistent full page, explainable live results, searchable filters and exact navigation restore.

## System contract

- Owns shared bounded query/filter/sort primitives, Talent Search, the player-company Roster workspace,
  saved views, shortlists and blacklist.
- Consumes canonical worker projections and knowledge visibility; it never owns duplicated worker data.
- Provides a registration boundary for later searchable domains without implementing their records.
- Emits paged results and navigation/selection state, not simulation or scouting truth.
- Keeps Talent Search and Roster as distinct destinations: they may share query infrastructure but not
  labels, default filters or player intent.

## Delivered execution

WM-021.1 combines the shared query/index contract, searchable include/exclude filters, stable
two-field sorting, SQL-side filtering/sorting/paging, configurable columns, four-worker comparison,
saved views, multiple shortlists, one blacklist and navigation restoration. History, regional
popularity, company, contract and Character criteria remain absent until their authoritative domains
exist. The current single `Worker.name` is not presented as a complete wrestling identity model.

WM-025 now supplies the identity model required to display active ring names and search dated aliases.
The current `Roster.tsx` alias of Talent Search is temporary and is not an acceptable company Roster.

## Remaining planned execution

[WM-021.2](WM-021.2.md) replaces the alias with a company-scoped Roster workspace. It uses canonical
facts available in the current save, displays active ring identities, supports useful sortable
columns and opens profile/compare/context actions without pretending that future brands, contracts,
roles or scouting facts already exist.

## Acceptance criteria

- The dedicated Search Workspace persists query, category, filters, sort, columns, selection, page
  and scroll state through profile navigation and Back.
- Text search prioritises names and aliases, supports bounded typo tolerance and explains non-obvious
  matches; it combines with every applicable filter.
- The query contract remains paged, bounded and server-backed; it does not replace the current
  UI with an in-memory world list.
- Search and filters work together and preserve an intentional view when the player navigates
  away and returns.
- Players can maintain named saved views plus explicit shortlists and blacklists; every list is
  searchable, sortable and reversible without changing the underlying worker record.
- Confirmed WM-020 base/sub-stat fields and WM-022 identity fields are filterable only through
  the canonical data model; no duplicate UI-only fields are introduced.
- The approved catalogue covers availability, employment/company history, relationship status and
  company/status criteria as well as identity and skill fields. Publicly demonstrated ability is
  exact for established workers; private and genuinely unknown information remains hidden.
- Results are deterministically sorted and paged, support configurable columns and comparison of up
  to four workers, and never expose hidden values through filtering.
- Name, Overall, each visible base stat, popularity, age and condition columns are sortable through
  keyboard-accessible table headers. Active direction and any secondary sort are visible, stable and
  restored with the search state.
- Empty states, invalid combinations, clear/reset behaviour and accessible keyboard use are
  explicit and tested.
- Tests cover query validation, representative combined filters, pagination, stable sorting and
  saved-view persistence.
- Roster lists the player's current company personnel by active ring identity, preserves its own
  query/filter/sort/navigation state and never silently behaves like all-world Talent Search.
- Roster exposes profile, compare and only those booking, interaction, contract or development
  actions whose owning command and permissions exist; future actions are absent rather than decorative.

## Foundation exit

Large current-data worker sets remain fast to search, filter, compare and revisit through canonical
known data, including reliable saved views and reversible shortlists/blacklists. WM-025 identity
integration is delivered; the parent capability exits when WM-021.2 provides a genuine company Roster.

# WM-021 — Roster discovery and filtering

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. A first roster slice depends on WM-020 and
WM-022; relationship filters later consume WM-023. Reserved branch stem:
`feature/wm-021-roster-discovery`. Blocking visibility decision: PD-102.

## Outcome and scope

Make the roster practical to explore at game scale through a TEW IX-inspired search and filtering
workflow. The player should be able to find wrestlers by a text search and combine relevant
profile, availability and stat criteria without loading the entire world into the browser.

"Like TEW IX" is a product reference, not a licence to copy its screens or assume its full filter
catalogue. The owner will decide which filters, sort orders, saved views and comparison behaviours
matter most before implementation.

## System contract

- Owns bounded roster query/filter/sort contracts, saved views, shortlists and blacklists.
- Consumes canonical worker projections and knowledge visibility; it never owns duplicated worker data.
- Emits paged results and navigation/selection state, not simulation or scouting truth.

## Planned execution slices

1. Approve filter catalogue/PD-102 and extend the bounded server-backed query contract.
2. Composable include/exclude filtering, stable sorting and paged accessible UI.
3. Saved views, shortlist/blacklist and navigation-state persistence.
4. Availability/history/relationship/company criteria, comparison and scale/performance evidence.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-102 and an owner-approved filter/sort/comparison catalogue are required before their UI slices.

## Acceptance criteria

- The ticket records an owner-approved filter catalogue, including which criteria can be combined,
  searched, sorted, excluded and saved as views.
- The query contract remains paged, bounded and server-backed; it does not replace the current
  UI with an in-memory world list.
- Search and filters work together and preserve an intentional view when the player navigates
  away and returns.
- Players can maintain named saved views plus explicit shortlists and blacklists; every list is
  searchable, sortable and reversible without changing the underlying worker record.
- Confirmed WM-020 base/sub-stat fields and WM-022 identity fields are filterable only through
  the canonical data model; no duplicate UI-only fields are introduced.
- The approved catalogue covers availability, employment/company history, relationship status and
  company/status criteria as well as identity and skill fields, with scouting uncertainty respected.
- Empty states, invalid combinations, clear/reset behaviour and accessible keyboard use are
  explicit and tested.
- Tests cover query validation, representative combined filters, pagination, stable sorting and
  saved-view persistence.

## Capability exit

Large rosters remain fast to search, filter, compare and revisit through canonical known data,
including reliable saved views and reversible shortlists/blacklists.

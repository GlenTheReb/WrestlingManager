## Outcome and scope

Introduce match-local participant slots and sides, participation and victory rules, and an
explicit booked result. A winning team and the wrestler taking the deciding fall are different
facts. The contract must represent singles, tag, trios, handicap, tornado, multi-person and
elimination bookings without claiming that their runtime mechanics already exist.

The existing singles plan remains the persisted and UI-facing format for this increment.
Convert it into the shared contract at validation boundaries. Do not introduce two editable
sources of truth or silently reinterpret old saves. WM-002/003 implement expanded simulation
and must version/migrate the persisted plan before accepting expanded bookings. WM-004 supplies
the booking interface.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every participant has a unique slot and worker identity and belongs to exactly one declared
  side. Every side is populated; there are at least two opposing sides. Match sides do not
  require a permanent team/stable record. Uneven teams remain representable.
- Participation rules distinguish tags from all-active matches; victory rules distinguish
  one fall from elimination. Structurally valid future formats are distinguishable from
  formats supported by the current simulator.
- A decisive result identifies the winning side. Pinfall/submission identify both deciding
  and defeated slots on opposing sides. Whole-side count-outs/disqualifications may omit
  individual actors. Draws and no contests cannot carry a winning side.
- Existing singles plans use this validation when saved, auto-planned and started. Unsupported
  match labels still fail clearly; future structures cannot be fed to the two-worker engine.
- Rust exports the new types to TypeScript from one definition. Legacy MatchPlan JSON,
  SQLite schema 3 and engine 0.2.0 snapshots remain unchanged.
- Tests cover valid and invalid team/result combinations, every existing finish, current
  engine capability checks, legacy deterministic output and save/resume compatibility.

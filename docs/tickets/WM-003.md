# WM-003 — Multi-person, elimination and timed entry

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. Depends on WM-002. Suggested delivery lane: M2.
Reserved branch stem: `feature/wm-003-multi-person`.

## Player promise

The player can book multi-side and survival structures whose entrants, falls, eliminations and
remaining competitors are genuine rules, not flavour attached to a singles simulation.

## System contract

- Owns active-competitor sets, entry order/time, fall/elimination records and survival result rules.
- Consumes WM-001 sides/results and WM-002 legal-worker/team runtime.
- Emits canonical entry, fall, elimination and survivor facts for reports, histories and presentation.
- WM-005 owns environmental/stipulation modifiers; WM-027 owns tournament brackets.

## Planned execution slices

1. Three/four-way one-fall matches with valid targeting and result invariants.
2. Elimination and handicap/gauntlet structures with dated fall history.
3. Battle-royal and configurable timed-entry runtime.
4. Booking UI, agent/live-instruction targeting, migration and deterministic resume coverage.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Active targets and winners must satisfy the current participation/victory rules at every tick.
- Entry and elimination order persist once and cannot be reconstructed from presentation text.
- Do not hard-code trademarked branded formats; compose participant, entry and victory rules.
- Do not add tournament scheduling or title consequences here.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-111 must approve supported fall types, draw/no-contest behavior, timed-entry controls, over-the-top-rope
semantics and how much elimination order the player may pre-book.

## Capability exit

Each supported structure validates, simulates, resumes and reports deterministically, including
invalid-target rejection and official surviving/winning participants.

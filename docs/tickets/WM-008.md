# WM-008 — Crowd, safety and long-term consequence balance

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. Depends on WM-007 and the accepted WM-020 stat
mapping plus PD-105. Suggested delivery lanes: M2–M4. Reserved branch stem:
`feature/wm-008-show-consequences`.

## Player promise

Different workers, products, audiences and booking choices create understandable tradeoffs in crowd
response, execution, safety, development, wear, confidence and future drawing power.

## System contract

- Owns calibrated mappings and causal explanations, not the canonical identity of workers/products.
- Consumes WM-020 sub-stats, match/angle facts, regional audience/product context and history.
- Emits separate consequence dimensions, dated worker/company changes and explanation factors.
- No context-free universal match score or universally optimal style is introduced. A report may
  show a 0–100 rating for how that performance landed with that show's actual audience/product, but
  it must retain execution, psychology, engagement, safety, story and protection breakdowns.

## Planned execution slices

1. Approved stat-to-simulation mapping with golden fixtures.
2. Product/region/audience expectation model after PD-105.
3. Safety, fatigue, injury, development and wear calibration.
4. Representative-card, season and long-save balance/property evidence.
5. Contextual live-change consequences spanning cohesion, safety, crowd response, worker comfort and
   downstream morale/relationship/promise evidence without duplicating those owning systems.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Tuning constants are named, bounded, tested and explained; hidden arbitrary bonuses are rejected.
- Reports keep execution, psychology, engagement, safety, story and protection distinct.
- WM's show-context rating, journalist reviews and fan ratings are separate facts with separate
  causes; no later opinion silently replaces the simulation result.
- Balance work cannot silently change deterministic engine behavior without a version decision.
- Do not tune from one anecdotal show or make one wrestling style globally superior.
- Last-minute direction is not automatically harmful. Urgency, preparation, instruction load,
  acknowledgement, worker fit and actual execution determine any consequence.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-101, PD-105, PD-112 and PD-134 govern inputs. The execution ticket must define target distributions,
acceptable injury/wear ranges and the career horizon used as balance evidence.

## Capability exit

Representative short and long careers show believable, diagnosable tradeoffs with bounded outcomes
and no dominant universal booking/product strategy.

# WM-028 — Storylines, creative planner and pre-booking

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. Depends on WM-025–027. Suggested delivery lane:
M3. Reserved branch stem: `feature/wm-028-storyline-planner`.

## Player promise

The player can plan feuds and long arcs with objectives, future beats and unresolved hooks while the
actual show, worker availability and audience response remain capable of changing the plan.

## System contract

- Owns storyline identity, participants/roles, objectives, planned beats, callbacks and state/history.
- Consumes characters/groups/titles, calendar availability, promises and canonical show events.
- Emits pre-booking conflicts, creative context, progression/disruption facts and future-plan views.
- It guides booking; it never rewrites official results to force a planned arc.

## Planned execution slices

1. Storyline/participant/objective schema and dated creative memory.
2. Pre-booked future match/angle/title path with availability/rule conflict checks.
3. Actual-event progression, disruption, callbacks and unresolved-hook suggestions.
4. Calendar/profile/booking/news integration plus multi-month regression fixture.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Planned, announced and completed events are distinct states.
- Only canonical show events advance history; deleting a plan cannot delete what happened.
- Kayfabe story state cannot silently change real relationships or contract promises.
- Do not create scripted dialogue/scene runtime; WM-029 owns that.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-124 must approve storyline objective/state vocabulary, automatic progression limits and visibility of announced
versus private plans.

## Capability exit

A multi-month story can be planned, disrupted, resumed and audited from its actual event history with
useful conflict warnings and no forced outcomes.

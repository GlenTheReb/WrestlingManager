# WM-012 — Fictional world generation and world-state foundation

Status: Incremental capability brief; not implementation-ready wholesale. A career-bootstrap slice
follows the existing Person/Character rules and a thin WM-011 company identity; later relationship
completion consumes WM-031–034 and WM-042. Suggested delivery lanes: M1 and M5. Reserved branch stem:
`feature/wm-012-world-foundation`.

## Player promise

The player can create a detailed fictional industry without entering a seed, understand its size and
performance cost before starting, and later inspect how its companies and relationships evolved.

## System contract

- Owns deterministic fictional-world generation, automatic/advanced seeds, regional density presets,
  generation preview/manifest, canonical company-to-company sentiment, markets/eras and dated events.
- Consumes Person ratings/personality, Character identity, company identity and validated database
  content; WM-032 owns later newgens and WM-041 owns imported package validation.
- Emits one valid career bootstrap plus shared decision/report inputs and searchable causal history.
- Corporate agreements/actions are WM-037; policy/behavior AI is WM-038.

## Planned execution slices

1. Automatic seed, Small/Standard/Large/Realistic presets, all-region coverage and a deterministic
   generation preview with people/company/history/save-size and measured performance estimates.
2. Coherent generated people, companies, staff, identities, culturally plausible legal/ring names,
   careers, relationships and initial histories using existing canonical models.
3. Versioned world-event envelope, stable entity references and correction/supersession rules.
4. Directional company sentiment, ownership history, market/era context and bounded world queries.
5. Player/CPU-compatible decision and explanation contracts plus long-career compaction evidence.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Events are append-once or explicitly superseded; history is never rewritten silently.
- Relationships may be directional and date-effective; ownership has explicit authority and shares.
- CPU actors cannot read hidden player data or bypass finance/contracts.
- A seed is generated automatically and hidden in normal setup; Advanced Setup may expose it for
  reproducibility. The same database, seed, settings and engine version reproduce the bootstrap.
- Every supported region exists in every preset; presets change density/detail rather than erasing
  parts of the world. Generated records must satisfy the same validation as imported content.
- Foundational generation settings become immutable after career creation except through audited
  Sandbox editing. Preview estimates are labelled estimates and derived from measured fixtures.
- Do not implement alliances, takeovers or booking AI inside the foundation.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-134 rules A2, C3–C8, F1–F4, G1–G7, T2/T6 and U1–U2 lock generation, world granularity and long-save
direction. Each execution ticket must specify bounded catalogues, measured preview fixtures,
retention policy and migration/soak evidence without inventing demographic probabilities.

## Capability exit

A generated career is valid, reproducible and internally coherent at every preset; world events and
relationships remain deterministic and queryable for player and CPU systems without parallel models.

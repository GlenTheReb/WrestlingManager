# WM-012 — Company/world relationship foundation

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. A schema/event slice follows WM-011 and WM-042;
full completion consumes WM-031–034. Suggested delivery lane: M5. Reserved branch stem:
`feature/wm-012-world-foundation`.

## Player promise

The industry changes through dated, inspectable company decisions and relationships rather than
unrelated random headlines or CPU-only shortcuts.

## System contract

- Owns canonical company-to-company sentiment, ownership edges, markets/eras and dated world events.
- Consumes company, finance, contract, event and broadcast facts.
- Emits shared decision/report inputs for player and CPU companies plus searchable causal history.
- Corporate agreements/actions are WM-037; policy/behavior AI is WM-038.

## Planned execution slices

1. Versioned world-event envelope, stable entity references and correction/supersession rules.
2. Directional company sentiment and ownership edge history.
3. Market/era context and bounded world queries.
4. Player/CPU-compatible decision and explanation contracts.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Events are append-once or explicitly superseded; history is never rewritten silently.
- Relationships may be directional and date-effective; ownership has explicit authority and shares.
- CPU actors cannot read hidden player data or bypass finance/contracts.
- Do not implement alliances, takeovers or booking AI inside the foundation.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-130 must approve market/era granularity, relationship dimensions and retention/compaction rules for long saves.

## Capability exit

The world has deterministic, queryable event and relationship foundations that both player and CPU
systems can use without parallel models.

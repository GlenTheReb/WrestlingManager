# WM-006 — Rule-aware road-agent planning and live adaptation

Status: Capability brief; PD-112 decision-complete but not implementation-ready. Depends on WM-005.
Suggested delivery lane: M2.
Reserved branch stem: `feature/wm-006-road-agent-rules`.

## Player promise

Road agents behave like knowledgeable collaborators: they propose a reviewable structure suited to
the workers, result, rules, audience and risk, then interpret legal live changes without overriding
the player's locked creative intent.

## System contract

- Owns deterministic advice generation, pre-match worker consultation, instruction translation,
  referee delivery, participant response and rule-aware live adaptation.
- Consumes worker/agent knowledge, plans, current match state and WM-005 legality/risk.
- Emits editable proposed beats, worker concerns/alternatives/refusals and the complete delayed live
  instruction lifecycle defined by PD-112.
- It never owns official results, permanent worker relationships or presentation prose.

## Planned execution slices

1. Explainable suggestions for team roles and representative rules.
2. Structure/pacing/risk planning that preserves fixed manual beats and the booked winner.
3. Sequence-opportunity-driven referee relay, worker response and rule-aware future-plan adaptation.
4. Knowledge/trust/morale/ego/safety effects plus deterministic/property and UI review tests.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Advice is a proposal until accepted; automatic generation never erases manual work.
- The same inputs, instruction IDs and seed produce the same advice, delivery opportunity and response.
- Agents cannot authorize illegal actions or secretly change a booked result. Only an explicit
  player-authorised amendment may change a future finish or winner.
- Most workers comply with reasonable direction; every concern, imperfect response or refusal needs an
  explainable contextual cause. There is no arbitrary disobedience roll or fixed instruction quota.
- Do not add runtime generative models or generic unexplained “agent quality” bonuses.

## Decisions before relevant slices

Follow accepted [PD-112](../live-match-direction-rules.md). Exact balance constants and content breadth
remain execution-ticket decisions supported by representative fixtures rather than invented percentages.

## Capability exit

Agents produce reviewable, differentiated and rule-correct plans whose reasoning and later execution
can be inspected without contradicting official results.

# WM-024 — Person profile and career hub

Status: Decision-complete capability brief under accepted PD-133; implementation is not authorised.
The existing thin profile follows WM-020, WM-022, WM-023 and local WM-025.1. Full depth consumes later
canonical career systems as they land. Reserved branch stem: `feature/wm-024-person-profile-hub`.

## Player promise

One original WM information hub answers who a person is, what they can do across roles, how they are
doing, where they have been and what the player can legitimately do next.

## System contract

- Owns profile composition/navigation and contextual action eligibility, not duplicate worker data.
- Consumes canonical stats, identity, knowledge, relationships, contract, health, moveset and dated
  career/history projections from their owning systems.
- Emits deep links and explicit commands; opening a profile never mutates state.
- Long histories are paged and private/unknown facts respect PD-102.
- Uses the complete accepted contract in `docs/person-profile-hub-rules.md` (PD-133).

## One-ticket internal implementation phases

1. Persistent full-page/quick-reference shell, dense Overview and contextual Actions menu.
2. Role-aware Attributes, Character, personal facts, relationships, condition and knowledge states.
3. Paged career/match/appearance projections, navigation restoration, comparison and Media rail.
4. Moveset/development/contract extension points backed only by currently canonical data, plus
   accessibility, performance and native smoke verification.

These are internal phases on one execution ticket and branch, not separate owner-prompt checkpoints.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Each field has one source owner and deep-links to evidence/history where useful.
- Unknown is represented as unknown, never as zero or fabricated certainty.
- Actions explain invalid/permission/cooldown states and call owning domain commands.
- Do not create a second context-free wrestler Overall or load the entire world history at once.
- Match history exposes date, company/show, participants, result and finish, match type/stipulation,
  title context, duration, show-context rating and its dimensions, crowd response and recorded
  incidents when those canonical facts exist.
- Popularity is separate from wrestling ability and current-style Overall. The profile leads with the
  relevant market's 0–100 value and lets the player inspect regional values; momentum, marketability
  and company reach remain separate.
- Related news, show reports, companies and opponents deep-link without losing the active profile tab
  or history position.
- Every Person has canonical wrestling ratings even when currently used outside the ring; profession
  ratings remain separate and unknown is reserved for genuinely unsupported knowledge.
- Attribute values use the accepted red-to-green quality scale. Objective match dimensions and
  attributed subjective journalist reviews can never appear as one rating.
- Protected-move permission is a contextual social/creative interaction distinct from move safety;
  WM-010/023/031/032 own the relevant facts and consequences.

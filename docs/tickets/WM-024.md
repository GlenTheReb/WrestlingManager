# WM-024 — Wrestler profile and career hub

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. The existing thin profile follows WM-020 and
WM-022; full capability consumes the WM-021 foundation, WM-023, WM-025 and later systems. Reserved branch stem:
`feature/wm-024-wrestler-profile`.

## Player promise

One FM-style profile answers who a wrestler is, what they can do, how they are doing, where they
have been and what the player can legitimately do next.

## System contract

- Owns profile composition/navigation and contextual action eligibility, not duplicate worker data.
- Consumes canonical stats, identity, knowledge, relationships, contract, health, moveset and dated
  career/history projections from their owning systems.
- Emits deep links and explicit commands; opening a profile never mutates state.
- Long histories are paged and private/unknown facts respect PD-102.

## Planned execution slices

1. Identity/portrait, six-group stats and explainable sub-stat profile summary.
2. Condition, morale, contract/company and contextual actions.
3. Relationships, personality, teams/stables, titles and moveset tabs.
4. Detailed old matches, company/character history, awards and recent news with pagination.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Each field has one source owner and deep-links to evidence/history where useful.
- Unknown is represented as unknown, never as zero or fabricated certainty.
- Actions explain invalid/permission/cooldown states and call owning domain commands.
- Do not create a second context-free wrestler Overall or load the entire world history at once.
- Match history exposes date, company/show, participants, result and finish, match type/stipulation,
  title context, duration, show-context rating and its dimensions, crowd response and recorded
  incidents when those canonical facts exist.
- Popularity is separate from wrestling ability and current-style Overall. The profile leads with the
  relevant market's 0–100 value and lets the player inspect regional values; momentum, marketability
  and company reach remain separate.
- Related news, show reports, companies and opponents deep-link without losing the active profile tab
  or history position.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-101–104 and accepted PD-106 are required. WM-025's core Person/Character model must precede final
identity, character history and wrestling-surface integration; a profile must not cement the legacy
single-name Worker model.

## Capability exit

The profile remains responsive and coherent across new, veteran, unemployed, injured, hidden and
history-heavy workers, with all displayed facts matching canonical sources.

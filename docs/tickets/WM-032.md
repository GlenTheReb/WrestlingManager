# WM-032 — Staff, facilities, scouting and development

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. The unified Person/profession model is accepted
as PD-009 and wrestler development inputs as PD-101. Remaining dependencies are WM-020, WM-022,
WM-011, WM-030–031 and WM-042. Suggested delivery lane: M4. Reserved branch stem:
`feature/wm-032-development`.

## Player promise

The player builds organisational capability through staff and facilities, discovers uncertain talent
and develops people through explainable plans, mentoring, experience and recovery.

## System contract

- Owns Shared Person attributes, Profession Skills, Role Experience, the common Role Rating kernel,
  staff roles/assignments/workload, facility levels, scouting reports/knowledge and development plans.
- Consumes worker stats/potential/identity, contracts, finance, training/match/recovery history.
- Emits dated knowledge estimates, Role Ratings, development changes, staff/facility effects and office events.
- WM-010 owns move learning; WM-031 owns employment; WM-033 owns scheduled event experience.

## Planned execution slices

1. Shared Person/Profession Skill types, role-weight configuration and deterministic Role Rating.
2. Staff roles, assignments, workload and facility investment/cost integration.
3. Knowledge model and scouting reports respecting PD-102.
4. Individual training, recovery and mentoring plans with explainable progress.
5. Schools/academies, try-outs, newgens, call-ups, excursions/loans, retirement and staff conversion.

## Accepted Profession Skill catalogue — PD-009

- Broadcasting: Play-by-Play, Colour Commentary, Interviewing, Hosting.
- Creative: Booking, Long-Term Planning, Talent Evaluation, Match Production, Angle Production.
- Coaching: Technical Coaching, Performance Coaching, Physical Conditioning, Youth Development.
- Officiating: Refereeing.
- Scouting: Current Ability Judgment, Potential Judgment, Regional Knowledge.
- Medical: Medicine, Physiotherapy, Rehabilitation.
- Business: Negotiation, Finance, Marketing, Sponsorship, Broadcast Relations.
- Role-specific responsibilities may add focused skills such as Ringside Management, Client
  Promotion or Segment Improvisation when they do not duplicate Entertainment attributes.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Unknown ability/potential remains uncertain; scouting never reveals hidden truth automatically.
- Development cites age, potential, trainer, facility, plan, matches and recovery factors.
- Facility spending and staff wages reconcile with WM-042.
- Do not guarantee linear growth or make one plan optimal for every worker.
- One Person may hold wrestling and multiple Profession roles. Missing wrestling ability stays absent;
  Entertainment is shared for on-screen work and must not be duplicated as job-specific microphone stats.
- `Role Rating = 65% relevant Profession Skills + 20% Shared Person attributes + 15% Role Experience`.
  Actual performance may additionally consume workload, chemistry, product knowledge, language,
  pressure and fatigue through the responsible capability's documented rules.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-101 and the PD-009 Person/profession kernel are accepted. PD-102–103 and PD-107 remain required.
Approve role-specific skill weights/responsibilities, facility categories, potential visibility,
development cadence and newgen demographic/content rules before their corresponding slices.

## Capability exit

Scouting and development create meaningful uncertain choices, bounded long-term outcomes and complete
financial/history explanations across worker life-cycle transitions.

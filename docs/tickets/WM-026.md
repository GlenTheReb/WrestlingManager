# WM-026 — Teams, stables, managers and authority figures

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. Depends on WM-025; match-local team mechanics
come from WM-002. Suggested delivery lane: M3. Reserved branch stem: `feature/wm-026-groups`.

## Player promise

Tag teams, trios, stables, managers, valets and on-screen authority persist as creative entities with
membership, roles, chemistry, loyalty and history—not temporary labels on a match.

## System contract

- Owns dated group identity/membership/roles, group-level chemistry/loyalty and kayfabe authority.
- Consumes people/characters, company/brand/division context and personal relationship projections.
- Emits eligibility/context for booking, storylines, profiles, matches and histories.
- WM-002 owns legal match sides; WM-030 owns real company management authority.

## Planned execution slices

1. Permanent team/group schema with dated membership and role history.
2. Tag/trios integration and team chemistry history without conflating match-local sides.
3. Stables/factions, leadership, loyalty and breakup/turn events.
4. Managers/valets/on-screen authority plus profile/search/history UI.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Group membership is date-effective and never erases former affiliations.
- Kayfabe loyalty/chemistry cannot silently create personal friendship or employment authority.
- Match composition references a group optionally but remains valid without one.
- Do not add titles, tournaments or corporate reporting lines.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-134 rules L1–L5 lock group types, overlapping membership and group qualities. The execution ticket
must define the first permission and eligibility matrices with representative histories.

## Capability exit

Groups have coherent identities and histories, affect valid creative choices and integrate with tag
simulation without becoming a second source of participant truth.

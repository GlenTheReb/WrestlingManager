# WM-035 — Company office and actionable inbox

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. A thin M1 office consumes WM-030–031 and WM-042;
later slices integrate WM-032–034 and beyond. Reserved branch stem: `feature/wm-035-office`.

## Player promise

The office tells the player what changed, why it matters, when action is due and where to act—without
requiring a tour of disconnected screens or presenting meaningless dashboard totals.

## System contract

- Owns alert aggregation/deduplication, inbox/diary projections, quick-action routing and resolution state.
- Owns personalised career onboarding presentation: formal email for official communication and an
  informal phone/text timeline for worker/staff conversations, both backed by canonical history.
- Consumes canonical events, deadlines, permissions and actions from owning systems.
- Emits read/resolved/snoozed state and navigation; it does not own the underlying business fact.
- Shortlist/blacklist data remains WM-021; the office may expose shortcuts to those lists.

## Planned execution slices

1. Typed actionable-message contract extending the existing news/inbox foundation.
2. Role/company/world-specific welcome, assigned advisor introductions, expectations and first tasks
   consuming the WM-030 player career and governance facts.
3. Company snapshot, assistant priorities and cause/deadline/action deep links.
4. Formal email, informal phone/text conversations, diary, birthdays and contract/finance/event deadlines.
5. Delegation policies, deduplication, snooze/resolution history, workload prioritisation and
   accessibility journeys.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Messages derive from stored facts or explicit scheduled rules and identify their source.
- Reading, resolving or snoozing a message never resolves the underlying domain obligation.
- Alerts deduplicate without deleting history and respect player role/knowledge.
- Do not add a metric or quick button without a real explanation and command destination.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-134 rules R1–R6 lock office, communication and delegation direction. The execution ticket must
define its first urgency, authority, review, notification and diary fixtures.

## Capability exit

Representative weekly management issues are prioritized, explainable and actionable from the office,
and resolved items remain searchable historical evidence.

# WM-034 — Broadcasting, sponsors and production

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. Depends on WM-011, WM-033 and WM-042. Suggested
delivery lane: M4. Reserved branch stem: `feature/wm-034-broadcast-business`.

## Player promise

Distribution, sponsors and production create negotiated reach, revenue, expectations and obligations
rather than passive bonuses on a company screen.

## System contract

- Owns media/sponsor/ad deals, network/territory coverage, slots, ratings obligations, commentary
  assignments, merchandise programs and production plans.
- Consumes company/product/geo, event calendar/results, audience response and finance.
- Emits obligations, reach, revenue/cost ledger sources, trust changes and production/presentation cues.
- WM-036 owns journalists/social reaction; WM-013 carries show-view presentation metadata.

## Planned execution slices

1. Broadcast/streaming/PPV deal terms, coverage and event assignment.
2. Ratings/reach calculation with targets, exclusivity, renewal and network trust.
3. Sponsorship/advertising obligations and incident response.
4. Commentary/languages, merchandise and production investments/cues.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Every payment/cost reconciles with WM-042 and every reach/ratings result exposes its causes.
- Deals enforce territory, slot, exclusivity, content and production obligations.
- Production quality changes cost/expectation/presentation, not official match outcome.
- Do not model real networks, sponsors or media law without licensed/approved data.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-117 must approve deal/ratings abstractions, media territories, sponsor sensitivity, merchandise depth and
production categories.

## Capability exit

Deals and programs create enforceable, explainable business choices whose financial, audience,
trust and presentation consequences derive from actual scheduled shows.

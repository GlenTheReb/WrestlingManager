# WM-037 — Alliances, company groups and takeovers

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. Depends on WM-012, WM-030–034 and WM-042.
Suggested delivery lane: M5. Reserved branch stem: `feature/wm-037-company-networks`.

## Player promise

Promotions can cooperate, compete and change control through agreements, trades, groups and takeovers
whose rights, money, people and history remain explicit.

## System contract

- Owns alliances/working agreements, talent trades/loans, co-promotion, corporate grouping and
  competitive action records.
- Consumes company relationships, governance, contracts, finance, events and reputation.
- Emits enforceable rights/obligations, ownership transactions and dated relationship consequences.
- WM-038 decides CPU policy; this capability validates and applies chosen actions.

## Planned execution slices

1. Working agreement/alliance and co-promotion rights.
2. Talent trade/loan negotiation integrated with contracts and promises.
3. Parent/child/sister/developmental company and conglomerate structures.
4. Investment, merger, acquisition/takeover, rating battle and configurable competitive/dirty tactics.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Authority, worker contracts, cash and historical entity identity remain explicit after transactions.
- Aggressive actions carry documented legal abstraction, financial, relationship and reputation risk.
- A child/developmental company is not a brand; ownership/control edges are date-effective.
- Do not copy real antitrust/labour law or give CPU companies privileged transaction rules.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-134 rules T3–T5 lock competition, agreements and corporate-change direction. The execution ticket
must define the first legal transaction catalogue, thresholds, discovery risks and recovery paths.

## Capability exit

Representative agreements and ownership changes enforce rights, reconcile assets/obligations and
preserve inspectable company/worker histories for player and CPU actors.

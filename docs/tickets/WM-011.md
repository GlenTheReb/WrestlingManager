# WM-011 — Company identity and product foundation

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. A thin foundation slice may follow WM-020 for
M1; full completion integrates WM-025 and WM-029. Reserved branch stem:
`feature/wm-011-company-foundation`.

## Player promise

The player's promotion is a configurable organisation with its own identity, product, culture,
audience, brands and geographic position—not hard-coded UWF framing.

## System contract

- Owns canonical company identity, product/culture references, brand scope, size/prestige/momentum
  dimensions, headquarters and geographic reach.
- Consumes stable catalogues and worker/company IDs; later consumes kayfabe and angle context.
- Emits company context used by audience, contracts, finance, events, broadcasting, AI and UI.
- Ownership/governance is WM-030; money is WM-042; inter-company edges are WM-012/037.

## Planned execution slices

1. Stable company/product/culture/brand/geo schema and ordinary seeded UWF migration.
2. Career company-creation workflow with validation and honest defaults.
3. Product/audience/worker tradeoffs after PD-105.
4. Brand rosters/shows/titles/staff scoping and size/growth explanations.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Company size, prestige and momentum remain distinct causes; no single company overall score.
- A brand is a scoped part of one company, not an accidental second company record.
- Company identity cannot silently change contracts, ownership or historical event attribution.
- Do not copy another simulator's product taxonomy without owner approval.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-105 and PD-134 rules G1–G7 lock causal company/product direction. The execution ticket must define
its first creation options, brand scopes and mutable-field commands.

## Capability exit

Custom and seeded companies use the same validated model, and downstream systems can explain how
product, culture, size, brand and geography affect their decisions.

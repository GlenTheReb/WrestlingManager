# WM-042 — Company finance, budgets and investment

Status: Capability brief; not implementation-ready. A core ledger slice follows WM-011 and WM-030
for M1; later accounts integrate each business system. Reserved branch stem:
`feature/wm-042-company-finance`.

## Player promise

Every financial decision is understandable and reconcilable: the player can see cash, commitments,
budgets, forecasts, revenue/cost sources, debt/investment and the path into or out of distress.

## System contract

- Owns double-sided or equivalently auditable ledger entries, accounts/categories, budgets, forecasts,
  obligations, debt/investment and close/reconciliation rules.
- Consumes dated sources from contracts, events, facilities, training, media, merchandise and transactions.
- Emits balances, committed/available cash, statements, alerts and financial-history facts.
- Source systems authorize business actions; finance validates/posts their money exactly once.

## Planned execution slices

1. Currency-safe ledger/account model and migration from current simple finances.
2. Contract and event postings, period close and reconciled statements.
3. Budgets, commitments, forecasts and office explanations.
4. Facilities/media/merchandise postings, loans/debt, investments and distress/recovery.

## Invariants and exclusions

- Money uses integer minor units; every balance change has dated source/entity and idempotency identity.
- Statements reconcile to entries; no screen computes an independent “cash” truth.
- Difficulty may simplify information/automation but cannot change accounting identity silently.
- Do not invent tax/legal detail or turn bankruptcy into an unexplained game-over.

## Decisions before relevant slices

PD-134 rules Q6–Q9 lock finance complexity and distress direction. The execution ticket must define
accounting granularity, period cadence, starting-balance migration and forecast uncertainty.

## Capability exit

Representative seasons reconcile across contracts, events and business operations; budgets/forecasts
remain distinguishable from actuals, and distress/recovery has explicit tested rules.

# Wrestling Manager playable milestones

The 43 WM capability briefs describe the destination. Delivery follows vertical milestones so the
game remains playable and testable instead of completing one enormous subsystem at a time. A
milestone may take thin slices from several capabilities; those slices receive separate execution
tickets before implementation.

## M0 — Proven prototype — complete

Create/load a career, inspect a roster, book a singles show, receive road-agent assistance, watch
deterministic simulation, inspect consequences/news and continue time. WM-001 established the first
match-rule foundation and a pinned compatibility baseline.

## M1 — A management week worth repeating

The player understands wrestler strengths and identity, sees a coherent company and role, handles
one contract or relationship issue, books the existing match/angle card, sees an auditable financial
effect and receives actionable follow-up in the office.

Initial capability slices: WM-020–023, WM-025 before full WM-024, WM-011, WM-030–031, WM-035 and
WM-042. Use the
existing singles simulation rather than waiting for every match type. Exit only when the loop is
interesting for several consecutive in-game weeks and every consequence explains its cause.

## M2 — Teams, rules and watchable production

The player can book tag/trios and representative multi-person/stipulation structures, use improved
agent/finish tools and watch matches and angles through the first renderer-neutral text-led viewer.

Capability slices: WM-002–010, WM-013–014, WM-026 and the minimum WM-029 scene contract. Do not
complete every exotic rule before one tag match and one structured angle feel excellent.

## M3 — People and creative memory

Wrestlers feel like persistent people and distinct characters. Relationships, teams, titles,
storylines, promises, interactions and profile/history surfaces create decisions that persist across
shows without confusing real life and kayfabe.

Capability slices: WM-021–029, extending the M1 profile and M2 scene work. Exit requires an
inspectable multi-month feud, a relationship consequence and a character/team/title history.

## M4 — A company worth operating

The player manages staff, scouting, development, facilities, events, touring, house shows,
broadcasting, sponsors, production, media and finances through an assistant-led office. Systems share
one calendar, ledger, contract model and event history.

Capability slices: WM-011, WM-030–036 and WM-042. Exit requires a reconciled season, explainable
attendance/ratings and negotiations whose obligations affect later decisions.

## M5 — A living industry worth observing

CPU companies operate under compatible rules, form relationships and groups, trade talent, compete,
run inspectable shows and create searchable history, honours, alumni and legends.

Capability slices: WM-012 and WM-037–040. Exit requires deterministic multi-year simulation with
bounded performance and causal drill-down from world news to canonical events.

## M6 — A product worth extending and shipping

Saves recover safely, complex screens are coherent and accessible, performance/soak budgets pass,
and native custom scenarios can be validated and exchanged without requiring optional media packs.

Capability slices: WM-015–019 and WM-041. Exit requires a clean-machine release rehearsal and
honest documentation of implemented features, compatibility and limitations.

## M7 — Optional retro broadcast expansion

After the management game, renderer-neutral event contract and complete text/2.5D viewer are proven,
test whether a lightweight original PlayCanvas Engine v2 presentation through `@playcanvas/react` can
replay WM shows convincingly over a WebGL2 baseline. Begin
with WM-043's bounded one-arena/two-wrestler/five-move spike and stop if contact quality, asset rights,
performance or fallback behavior fails.

Capability slices: WM-013–014 and conditional WM-043, later consuming WM-007/010, WM-029, WM-034,
WM-041 and WM-018. Exit requires representative singles/tag playback across supported body types and
missing assets without changing any canonical result. M7 is optional and cannot block M1–M6 or release.

## Milestone rule

No milestone requires every capability in its list to be globally complete. It requires the smallest
connected slice that proves the promised player experience. Remaining scope stays visible in each
capability checklist and is scheduled only when evidence shows it improves the game.
